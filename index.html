About Us

A mother-daughter duo building an empire from the ground up.

Fivian is a homegrown Kolkata brand named after our family of five, turning over 15 years of culinary passion into a refined artisanal experience.

"Every item is handcrafted in small batches, preservative-free, authentic, and loaded with fresh local ingredients."

👩‍🍳 Culinary Excellence

Arshia Ahmad brings 15+ years of culinary experience, having showcased her craft across multiple television appearances. She is the artisan behind every product, handcrafting custom recipes and signature treats from scratch in small batches.

🎨 Brand, Story & Experience

Madeeha Anwar (MBA, Marketing) gives Fivian its soul and story. She designs the visual identity, custom packaging, brand personality, and designed and built this entire website from the ground up to craft a memorable customer experience.

Meet Our Twin Brand

Beyond artisanal sweets, we celebrate authentic Indian culinary roots through Flavoura. Dedicated to traditional savouries and timeless kitchen recipes, Flavoura brings you wholesome, rich flavours crafted to evoke the warmth of home.
