About Us

A mother-daughter duo built from the ground up.

Fivian is a homegrown brand born from a mother-daughter duo with a shared passion for culinary excellence and thoughtful design. Named after our family of five, Fivian was created to give a formal identity to Arshia Ahmad’s 15+ years of award-winning culinary expertise, turning proven artisanal recipes into an unmistakable gourmet brand.

Every single chocolate, truffle, and stuffed date is handcrafted, preservative-free, and made in small batches in our Kolkata workshop under Arshia's direct supervision. From unique flavour pairings like Rose-Pistachio, Paan, and Rasmalai to our take on the viral Kunafa chocolate bar, the magic inside every product comes from her kitchen mastery.

The visual story on the outside is shaped by her daughter, Madeeha Anwar, an MBA in Marketing who handles all branding, custom packaging, and visual design. Rooted in Make-in-India principles, Fivian offers an authentic, small-batch alternative to mass-produced sweets—delivering pure flavour, fresh local ingredients, and personalised gifting experiences.
